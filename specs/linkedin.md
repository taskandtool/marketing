## Thought Leader Ads (secondary source, unverified)

- Sponsors an existing post by a member (founder, employee). The post is the ad.
- No headline, no intro text, no CTA button. The post copy is all the copy.
- Not available for multi-image posts, polls, or documents. Text posts, single image, and video only.
- Compose these as organic posts first; see below.

## Organic posts (secondary source, unverified)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Hook | first 100 to 150 chars show before "see more"; the point must land there |
| Length | 1,300 to 2,000 chars |
| Paragraphs | 1 to 3 sentences each |
| Links | keep the post native; put the link in the first comment (about 80% of top posts carry no link) |
| Hashtags | 3 to 5, or none |
| Documents | 5 to 10 slides |
| Engagement weight | comments and saves count 3 to 5 times a like; end with a question |

The broetry tell: one line per paragraph, each a cliffhanger, building suspense to a cliché. Readers recognise it as generated. Fix: vary sentence length, use a single-line paragraph only for emphasis, tell a real story with primary research and a number. B2B voice is first-person operator, one insight per post, numbers as proof, question as CTA.
