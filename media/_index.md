title: Photos and clips
---

Your own photographs and clips: the work, the place, the product, the
people. The AI generates a picture only when none of these fits.

```
media/photos/   stills
media/clips/    video the owner filmed
media/_index.md this page: one line per file below, a photo shown
                (`![what it shows](photos/<file>)`), who is in it, whether it may be used in ads
```

Add files on the Files tab or in a chat message. The AI copies photos from your own site and profiles (`raw/`) here once it has checked them.

The AI puts only files marked OK for ads below in a creative. A photo of a recognisable person needs your OK, and the customer's too if it shows them or their home. The AI doesn't use photos of someone else's work.
