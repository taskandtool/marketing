title: Photos and clips
---

The business's own photographs and clips: the work, the place, the
product, the people. Real material beats anything a model can generate,
and it is the one thing only the owner has.

```
media/photos/   stills
media/clips/    video the owner filmed
media/_index.md this page: one line per file below, a photo shown
                (`![what it shows](photos/<file>)`), who is in it, whether it may be used in ads
```

Files arrive from the owner (the Files tab, a chat message), or from a
crawl of their own site or profiles (`raw/`), copied across once checked.

Nothing is used in a creative until its line below says it may
be: a recognisable person needs the owner's word, and the customer's when
it is their face or their home. A photograph of someone else's work is
never used.
