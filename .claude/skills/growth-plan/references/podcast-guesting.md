# Guest on podcasts

Put the owner on other people's shows. Each appearance borrows an audience
that already trusts the host, and leaves a recording to cut into posts.

## Fits

An owner with a story, an opinion and a few topics they can talk about for
forty minutes. Works for local businesses too, on local and trade shows.

## What it looks like

- Shows in the niche, with their hosts' emails (Rephonic; Listen Notes to
  find shows).
- A short pitch: who the owner is, their following, two or three topics
  that fit the show, and an offer to share the episode.
- After it airs, the recording turned into clips and posts.

## Tools

- Finding shows and hosts: a podcast database (Rephonic for hosts'
  emails, Listen Notes for search).
- The pitch: the `email` skill, sent as in `cold-email.md` or from the
  owner's own mailbox at a small volume; posts after: `social-post`.

## Watch

Appearances booked per hundred pitches, and visits and signups after each
episode.

## Repeats

A monthly job finds new shows, skips those already pitched (a record in
`lists/`), and drafts the pitches for the owner.

## Gotchas

- Pitch the show, not the business: the topics are for its listeners.
