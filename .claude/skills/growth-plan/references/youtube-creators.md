# YouTube creators

Pay creators who already teach the niche to make videos about the product
every month, with an affiliate cut on top. Their audience is the buyer, and
their videos can run as ads.

## Fits

A product that can be shown on screen and a niche with creators already
making tutorials or reviews in it.

## What it looks like

- Creators found by what they post and how their videos do, not their
  subscriber count.
- A monthly retainer for a set number of videos, plus an affiliate offer.
- The right to use their videos in the business's own ads, bought in the
  contract.

## Tools

- Finding and measuring creators: public counts through a scraper
  (ScrapeCreators, Apify).
- Outreach: the `email` skill. Affiliate tracking: the owner's affiliate
  tool.
- Their videos as ads: the `ad` skill.

## Watch

Views, affiliate signups and cost per signup, per creator per month.

## Repeats

A monthly job reads each creator's new videos and counts, and the
affiliate numbers, into `results.md`.

## Gotchas

- Paid videos are disclosed as ads on the platform; the contract says so.
- Contracts and payments are the owner's; the AI drafts, never agrees.
