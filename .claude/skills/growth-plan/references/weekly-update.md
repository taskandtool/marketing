# Weekly update email

Send the list an update every week at the same time: what is new, what
customers achieved, what is next. The steady rhythm makes the business look
like it is moving fast, and reminds the list it exists.

## Fits

A list of customers or signups, and something new most weeks.

## What it looks like

Tuesday morning, every week: one lead item (a feature, a job, a result),
two or three short ones, one link.

## Tools

The `email` skill (newsletter), sent from the owner's email tool; this
week's material from `creatives/`, `results.md` and what the owner says.

## Watch

Open and click rates over months, and replies.

## Repeats

A weekly job on Monday drafts Tuesday's email for the owner to approve.

## Gotchas

- A week with nothing new is skipped, not padded.
