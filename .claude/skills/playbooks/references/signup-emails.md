# Signup emails

A short sequence that starts the moment someone signs up, books or asks for
a quote, and turns them into someone who uses it or buys. Most people who
sign up never come back unless something brings them.

## Fits

Anything with a signup, trial, quote or booking to follow up.

## What it looks like

Cody's sequence:

- straight away: welcome
- an hour later: how to get started
- day 1: why the business built it, and who for
- day 2: what it is and what it does for them
- day 3: a check-in from the founder ("any questions?")
- day 4 onwards: what customers achieved, one story an email

Replies come to a real inbox, and an AI drafts the answers.

## Tools

- The sequence as a flow in the owner's email tool (Klaviyo, the CRM
  Starter App's follow-ups); the words through the `email` skill.
- Replies: drafts in the owner's mailbox (Gmail), for them to send.

## Watch

How many signups take the first real step, and how many reply.

## Repeats

A daily job drafts answers to new replies for the owner.

## Gotchas

- It goes only to people who signed up. Every email can be stopped.
