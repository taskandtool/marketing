# Short-form creator team

Hire a handful of short-form creators, each posting a video a day, then
find the formats that break out and have everyone make those. Volume finds
the outliers; sharing them makes the whole team better.

## Fits

A consumer product or app people can film themselves using, and a budget
for five to ten creators for a few months.

## What it looks like

- Five to ten creators, one video each a day, on their own accounts or the
  brand's.
- Every video tracked; the outliers (several times the usual views) picked
  out each week.
- The winning format written up and shared with all of them.

## Tools

- Tracking: a short-form tracker (Shortimize), or public counts through a
  scraper (ScrapeCreators, Apify).
- Briefs and hooks: the `copywriting` and `video` skills.

## Watch

Views per video, the share of outliers, and signups from the creators'
links or codes.

## Repeats

A weekly job reads every video's counts, names the outliers, and writes the
next week's brief from them.

## Gotchas

- Hiring and paying creators is the owner's; the AI writes the brief.
