# Ad creative at scale

Treat creative as the main lever: launch new ads every day, switch off the
losers, and make more of what wins. Ad platforms now pick the audience
themselves, so the ad's idea does the targeting.

## Fits

Ads already running with tracking in place, and enough budget that a new
ad gets a fair test. Without that, a test set of nine (the `ad` skill) is
the place to start.

## What it looks like

- Research what the buyer is in pain about and what they want, from sales
  calls, reviews and the `research` brief.
- Every ad built as hook, the future they want, how to get there, and the
  action. Statics and short clips, including person-to-camera clips.
- A small cohort of new ads into each ad set daily or weekly (Cody runs
  about five a day per ad set).
- Losers off by a rule the owner agreed (for example, cost per lead well
  above target after a set spend); winners get more budget and new hooks.
- Look at the winners as pictures, write down what they share, and brief
  the next cohort from that.
- Follow each ad to revenue: ad, visit, lead in the CRM, closed deal.

## Tools

- Making: the `ad`, `copywriting` and `video` skills; for person-to-camera
  clips, an avatar service (HeyGen) or a video model.
- Launching and switching off: Meta with ads management, or the owner in
  Ads Manager.
- Revenue: whatever CRM the business keeps (HubSpot, the CRM Starter App).

## Watch

Cost per lead and cost per closed customer, cohort against cohort. A
cheaper lead that never buys is not a win.

## Repeats

The cohort is the job: make the batch, put it up paused, send the owner one
message to approve, and on a schedule apply the agreed rule and report.
The rule, the target and every change go in `results.md` so the next run
knows what was done.

## Gotchas

- Distinct ideas, not twelve colourways of one; near-copies compete with
  each other.
- A realistic generated person must be labelled where the platform asks.
- Never switch on, raise a budget or delete without the owner. Pausing
  under the agreed rule is the one change a job may make, if the owner
  said so.
