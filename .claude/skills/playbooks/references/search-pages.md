# Search pages that convert

Write pages for the searches people make when they are about to buy: how
to do the job, the business against an alternative, alternatives to a
competitor, the service in a place. These searchers are close to deciding,
so a page that answers them well converts.

## Fits

People search for the problem, the category or a competitor's name. A local
business has its own version: the service plus the town.

## What it looks like

- Keywords near the purchase (how to, vs, alternatives, best, cost, near
  me), from DataForSEO or Semrush; Ahrefs if the owner has it.
- Each page written from what already ranks for that search, better and
  more specific, with a clear call to sign up or book.
- Every visitor pixelled for remarketing (`paid-ads.md`).
- Volume once it works: Cody runs about a hundred articles a month.

## Tools

- Research: a keyword source (DataForSEO, Semrush, Ahrefs) and Search
  Console for what already ranks.
- The pages: the Website app's blog or pages. This app writes the brief and
  the draft; the Website app publishes.

## Watch

Signups and bookings from those pages, in Search Console and Analytics,
over three to six months.

## Repeats

A weekly job picks the next searches, drafts the pages and hands them to
the owner, and a monthly one reads which pages bring signups.

## Gotchas

- Google penalises many pages made mainly to rank with little of the
  business's own knowledge in them. Each page needs the business's
  specifics: its prices, jobs, photos, opinions.
- Comparison pages state only what is true and current about the other
  product.
