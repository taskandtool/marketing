# Cold DMs

Message the right people directly on LinkedIn or X, in a steady daily
sequence. A short, specific note from a real profile gets read where an
email might not.

## Fits

Buyers who are active on LinkedIn or X, and an owner or salesperson whose
profile looks like someone worth answering.

## What it looks like

- A target list from the buyer profile: a search, a group, the engagers on
  a post (the owner's own posts first).
- A connection request with a reason, then one or two short messages that
  are about them.
- A daily amount the platform tolerates, every day, rather than bursts.

## Tools

- A LinkedIn and X automation tool (PhantomBuster), or an X DM inbox
  (InboxApp).
- The words: `copywriting`, in the owner's voice.

## Watch

Acceptance rate, reply rate and conversations started per week.

## Repeats

The tool runs the sequence; a weekly job reads the replies and drafts
answers for the owner.

## Gotchas

- LinkedIn and X limit and ban automated accounts. Say so, start below the
  tool's suggested daily limits, and let the owner decide.
- The owner's own profile is at stake; never their only account for work.
