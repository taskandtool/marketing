# Copywriting

Use this with the `writing` skill. An ad, a post or an email also needs a
hook that makes the right person stop, and words that fit the platform.
The voice is in `brand/voice.md`, what may be said in `claims.md`, and the
idea in `creative.md`.

## Hooks

The hook is the first line, the first second or the first frame. Write it
so the right reader stops and sees the piece is for them. Pick its type for
what the reader already knows (`references/hooks.md`). Write five to eight
in the customers' words from `research/audience.md`, keep the best two, and
record the rest in `research/hooks.md`. Put the offer in the first line, or
in the first three seconds of a video. Make a video understandable with the
sound off. In the body, give what the hook promised.

Draft: "Looking for reliable heating engineers in Leeds? Look no further!" <!-- tropes-allow -->

Rewrite: "Radiators cold at the top? Bleed them before you call anyone.
Here's how, in two minutes."

## Lengths

`specs/<platform>.md` has the limits and the visible cutoff. Where it gives
none: keep an ad headline under eight words, make the point before the
cutoff, and put twelve words or fewer on a picture.

## Checking

`python3 scripts/check.py <folder>` enforces the limits and runs the tropes
check. Then do the `writing` skill's cold read.
