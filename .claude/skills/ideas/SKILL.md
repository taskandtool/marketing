# Ideas

An idea is one argument for one reader at one stage of awareness. The best
come from evidence: customers' words, what competitors already claim, the
circumstance the customer is in, and proof the business can show.

## Steps

Copy and tick:

- [ ] 1. Read `brand/positioning.md`, `brand/audience.md`, `claims.md` and
      `research/brief.md`. With no research yet, say so and offer it; ideas
      without it are guesses.
- [ ] 2. Decide where the market sits (`references/frameworks.md`, market
      sophistication). If every competitor makes the same claim, a bigger
      version of it loses: lead with how this business does it, or who it
      is for.
- [ ] 3. Write ten to fifteen candidate ideas. Draw them from the sources
      below, each as one line: reader, stage, argument, the proof in
      `claims.md`.
- [ ] 4. Score each (below) and keep the top nine that differ in argument,
      not in colour or wording.
- [ ] 5. For each kept idea, create `creatives/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/creative.md`
      with `status: draft`, the angle, the stage, the framework, the claims,
      and the idea in two sentences. The hook comes next (the `hooks` skill).
- [ ] 6. Show the owner the nine as a short list, one line each, and ask
      which to make first.

## Where ideas come from

- **Customers' words**: their wants, dislikes and worries (`research/audience.md`).
- **Objections**: price, trust, timing, mess, "can I do it myself", "will
  they turn up". Each is an idea: the confession, the question answered,
  the comparison, the guarantee.
- **The circumstance**: what the customer is trying to get done and what
  else they might use instead (a fan heater and a video, not just another
  firm).
- **Competitor gaps**: the objection nobody answers, the angle nobody runs.
- **Proof the business has**: a real job, a number, a review.
- **The calendar**: the first cold week, a local event, a season.

## Scoring, before spending

Score each idea 0 to 2 on: specific to this business (a competitor could not
run it unchanged); proof exists in `claims.md`; fits the reader's stage; not
a smaller echo of what competitors say; the first frame or line both stops
and signals relevance; the body pays off the hook; it could have been posted
by a person; it differs from the others in the set; it can be made with what
the business has. This is judgement made explicit, not a validated model;
the market picks the winners.

## The test set

Three angles × three hooks or formats: nine pieces, distinct in argument.
When one wins, keep it and write new hooks for it; when one loses, change
its angle. Results go in `results.md`.
