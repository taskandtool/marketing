# The ad libraries

Checked October 2026 against practitioner guides and the scrapers' own
documentation. Meta's, Google's and LinkedIn's help pages did not load
then. Re-check a fact here before a decision rests on it.

| Library | Shows everywhere | Only for ads shown in the EU/EEA | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta Ad Library | creative, copy, CTA, page, start date, active status, platforms, "N ads use this creative and text"; filters for active, platform, language, media | reach and impression ranges, age and gender, targeting | inactive commercial ads disappear outside the EU; EU-delivered ads stay a year after their last impression |
| Google Ads Transparency Center | verified advertiser, format, surface (Search, YouTube, Shopping, Maps, Play), last shown, up to five variations, ad count | first shown, impression range, topic, audience | a year from last impression; two to three days late |
| LinkedIn Ad Library | creative, copy, advertiser, format; no login | first and last shown dates, impressions per country, targeting | ads since June 2023, a year after last impression |
| TikTok Ad Library (library.tiktok.com) | nothing outside Europe | advertiser search, first and last shown dates, audience and reach; covers the EU, the UK and Switzerland | a year after last shown |
| TikTok Creative Center (Top Ads) | likes, CTR percentile against the industry, budget tier, objective, industry, region, an engagement chart by the second | none | no dates; advertisers opt in; a login after the first few cards |

None shows spend, clicks, conversions or return for ordinary commercial
ads. They are for messaging research, not benchmarking.

## Reading each

- **Meta.** Search by page, not keyword, and filter to active. Outside the
  EU an inactive ad disappears. Read days running from active ads only. Read the landing page each ad points to; the offer is there.
- **Google.** Search the domain, open the verified advertiser's page,
  filter there and keep that URL; a domain search alone returns part of the
  set. A first-shown date (and so a run length) appears only with an EU
  region selected. There is no keyword search.
- **LinkedIn.** Read for format and message. Run dates show only for ads
  shown in the EU; outside it every ad looks the same age.
- **TikTok, by competitor.** The Ad Library has their ads only if they
  run in Europe; otherwise the Creative Center's top ads are the read.
- **TikTok Creative Center.** The 30-day default view is mostly new, small tests; switch to
  180 days for the proven set. The pool is opt-in, so absence means nothing.
- **A business that also sells in Europe:** run one extra pass with an EU
  region. It is the only way to see first-shown dates and reach.

## Run length

Under 14 days is a test; 21 to 45 is acceptable; 45 to 90 is strong;
90 and more is a staple. A long-running ad has probably not been
losing money. That makes it adequate, not the best. Variants, and the same
angle across competitors, mean more than age alone.

## Discount

Brand awareness, retargeting and app-install ads (evergreen by design);
dynamic catalogue ads (a system, not a concept); a large ad count on Google
(it may be many barely served variations).
